
# AeroShift — AI-Powered Drone Delivery Platform

AeroShift is a startup concept focused on transforming last-mile delivery through drone-assisted transportation, AI-powered operational coordination, and digital delivery verification. The platform envisions helping traditional delivery workers transition into drone operators while improving delivery efficiency and transparency.

## Live Demo

- **Live Website:** https://aeroshift-startup.vercel.app/
- **GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/prawin0614/Aeroshift-startup

## Problem Statement

Traditional last-mile delivery faces challenges such as traffic congestion, delivery delays, operational inefficiencies, and difficulties coordinating delivery activities. AeroShift explores drone-assisted delivery as an alternative approach to addressing these challenges.

## Proposed Solution

AeroShift aims to connect customers, delivery operations, and drone operators through a coordinated digital platform.

- **AI-Assisted Coordination:** Explore intelligent coordination of delivery tasks and operational workflows.
- **Drone-Assisted Delivery:** Support the concept of transporting eligible packages using drones.
- **Delivery Worker Transformation:** Enable delivery workers to explore opportunities as drone operators.
- **Delivery Tracking:** Provide visibility into order progress and delivery status.
- **Digital Verification:** Explore proof-of-delivery and payment-status verification.

## Key Features and Objectives

- Drone-assisted last-mile delivery concept.
- AI-driven delivery coordination.
- Digital order and delivery-status management.
- Delivery verification and payment-status tracking.
- A human-supervised operational model.
- A scalable approach to future delivery automation.

## How It Works

1. A customer places a delivery order.
2. The delivery workflow is initiated and coordinated.
3. An eligible delivery task is assigned through the proposed operational workflow.
4. A drone operator supervises the delivery process where permitted.
5. Delivery completion and payment status can be verified digitally.

*The workflow above describes the proposed system and does not imply that every feature is currently implemented.*
